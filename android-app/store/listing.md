## Short description (80 characters max)
Take a quick taste quiz, circle gift ideas, rank your list and share it.

## Full description
Wishwell turns "I don't know, surprise me" into a list your family can actually shop from.

Take a quick taste quiz: pick between pairs, choose what you're into, set a budget. Wishwell fishes out gift ideas that fit. Circle the good ones, cut the rest, then rank your keepers so everyone knows which one you want most.

When it's ready, copy a share link or the list as text and send it to family. They see your list in order and can mark things as bought so nobody doubles up.

Running a gift exchange for a club, team or family? Add everyone's name and Wishwell draws names for you, with a spending limit.

Free, no account, no ads. Your lists stay on your phone.

Prices shown are rough estimates. Buy links open a store search in your browser.

For ages 13 and up.
