Magic Coupons

Start with each store's own deals page, then RetailMeNot or Slickdeals. Save a code here, try it at checkout, and tap It worked! if it did. Codes are never called working until you say so.

Codes you share show up for everyone, with only the store, the code, what it's for and an end date. Codes shared by other shoppers are marked, and they're hidden once more people say they don't work.
