Your top picks

The white dot is you. It starts from your quiz answers and budget, moves toward ideas you circle or star, and away from ones you cut. The closest ideas are your picks, joined by gold lines. The map also reshapes itself after every pick: whatever your circles and stars have in common, and whatever sets your cuts apart, gets more room, so ideas spread out along what matters to you. Picks you make on your Wishwell list count here too.

Your taste so far

    What the directions mean

      Each idea gets a score for its kind of gift, interests, age fit, price, hands-on, social, lasting, outdoor and skill level. The map squeezes all of those into three directions, so ideas that are alike sit close together. Circles, stars, cuts and skips on this page stay on this page; your Wishwell list doesn't change.
