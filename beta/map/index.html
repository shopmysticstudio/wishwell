Your top picks

The white dot is you. It starts from your quiz answers and budget, moves toward ideas you circle or star, and away from ones you cut. The closest ideas are your picks, joined by gold lines.

    What the directions mean

      Each idea gets a score for its kind of gift, interests, age fit, price, hands-on, social, lasting, outdoor and skill level. The map squeezes all of those into three directions, so ideas that are alike sit close together. Circles, stars and cuts on this page stay on this page; your Wishwell list doesn't change.
